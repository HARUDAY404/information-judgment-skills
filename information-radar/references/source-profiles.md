# Source Profiles

Choose by the question's evidentiary need. Combine profiles when necessary and say what each source type can and cannot establish.

## Community needs and lived experience

Use public community posts, comments, interviews, surveys, and relevant population data. Community content can establish that an experience or language exists; it cannot alone estimate prevalence in a population.

## Scientific or causal questions

Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses, preregistered studies, peer-reviewed primary research, official statistics, and disclosed methods. Practitioner accounts may suggest hypotheses but do not establish causality.

## Product and technical change

Use official documentation, changelogs, releases, repositories, and status pages to establish what changed. Add reproducible independent tests to evaluate real behavior and limitations.

## Market and industry trend

Combine official statistics, filings, platform data, disclosed industry research, multiple market participants, and failure cases. Record geography, time window, sample, commercial incentives, and conflicting definitions.

## Creator and content analysis

Use structured platform samples, original posts, account history, median performance, comments, and non-success cases. Do not infer exposure, conversion, income, or causality without the required denominator or direct evidence.

## Personal method and reproducibility

Prefer full process records, costs, prior advantages, failures, comparable replications, and observable results. A truthful case proves occurrence under its conditions, not general reproducibility.

## Source roles

- `primary`: closest available evidence or original record;
- `independent`: not derived from the same origin and without shared material incentives;
- `interpretive`: useful expert or practitioner explanation;
- `community`: experience, language, demand, or weak-signal evidence;
- `opposing`: credible evidence or explanation that challenges the emerging view;
- `promotional`: may contain evidence but has a material conversion incentive.
